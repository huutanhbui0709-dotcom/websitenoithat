## Brand & Style
The design system articulates an aura of high-end architectural distinction, tailored specifically for elite residential spaces such as Vinhomes apartments and bespoke villas. The aesthetic synthesizes Modern Luxury with refined Indochine sensibilities and pure Minimalist restraint. 

The visual tone relies on architectural proportions: abundant negative space, restrained line weights, serene materials, and meticulous editorial cadence. The interface must feel like flipping through an exclusive haute couture architectural monograph: calm, stately, and unconditionally trustworthy.

## Colors
The palette balances tactile architectural warmth with dramatic luxury accents:

- **Canvas & Base (`#F8F6F2`)**: Warm ivory/alabaster base evoking natural linen, limestone, and unbleached silk.
- **Surface Elevation (`#E8E4DC`)**: Warm sand gray used for subtle structural divides, secondary cards, and interior backdrop layers.
- **Primary Regal Accent (`#B08D57`)**: Champagne gold providing an understated metallic prestige for key focal elements, active states, and refined emblems.
- **Vibrant Accent (`#D4AF37`)**: Warm gold highlight reserved for micro-accents, VIP badges, and delicate hovered indicators.
- **Deep Architectural Anchor (`#121212` & `#1A1A1A`)**: Obsidian and charcoal providing structural contrast, high-impact hero sections, and crisp editorial typography.
- **Natural Harmony (`#3B4B3F`)**: Indochine-inspired muted moss/deep olive used selectively for botanical cues, sustainability markers, and grounded secondary badges.

## Typography
The typographic architecture pairs the classical literary elegance of **Playfair Display** with the technical clarity and native Vietnamese diacritic support of **Be Vietnam Pro**.

Headlines should never be set in heavy, loud weights; medium and semi-bold weights preserve sculptural elegance. All labels and category subheads must use uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.15em` to `0.2em`) to establish an haute-couture editorial presence. Body text preserves generous line heights to facilitate effortless reading of technical specifications, spatial narratives, and project briefs.

## Layout & Spacing
A 12-column responsive fluid grid governs desktop layouts, transitioning to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile devices.

Section margins are intentionally generous (`margin`: 3.5rem desktop), allowing architectural imagery to breathe without crowding interface controls. Content layouts mimic editorial spreads: asymmetric column spans (e.g., 5 columns copy against 7 columns imagery) are encouraged to evoke bespoke craftsmanship. Element gaps inside components strictly adhere to the 4px baseline rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is constructed through material layering rather than physical drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers**: Base ivory canvas (`#F8F6F2`) hosts raised content containers in solid pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or warm sand (`#E8E4DC`). Dark narrative sections transition deliberately into midnight obsidian (`#121212` and `#1A1A1A`).
- **Hairline Outlines**: Elements demarcate boundaries via ultra-fine 1px borders in `#E8E4DC` (light containers) or `#B08D57` at 20% opacity (dark containers), avoiding visual clutter.
- **Ambient Floor Shadows**: When floating modals or dropdown menus require separation, use an ultra-diffused ambient shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.06)`. Heavy, dark drop shadows are strictly prohibited.

## Shapes
Geometry is disciplined, crisp, and contemporary. Elements maintain a Soft (`roundedness: 1` — 4px standard radius) edge treatment to preserve the architectural precision of bespoke millwork and stonework. 

Overly rounded, bubbly, or pill geometries are forbidden for primary surfaces and buttons, as they diminish the tailored luxury aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#1A1A1A` background with crisp white typography, styled with 4px border radius, uppercase typography, and wide letter-spacing (`label-lg`). Hover state shifts background gracefully to `#B08D57`.
- **Secondary Action**: Transparent background with a 1px border in `#B08D57`, featuring champagne gold text. Hover fills background with `#B08D57` at 10% opacity.
- **Text Links**: Playfair or Be Vietnam Pro with an animated 1px hairline underline offset by 4px.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- Cards use an unbordered or hairline-bordered layout (`#E8E4DC`).
- Images feature a 4:5 or 16:9 architectural aspect ratio. On hover, the image scales smoothly (`scale(1.02)`) over 600ms ease-out.
- Project details are accompanied by high-tracking subheadings (`label-sm`) in `#B08D57` and titles in Playfair Display.

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Inputs**: Minimalist underline or clean rectangular frames with a 1px border (`#E8E4DC`). Focus state transitions the border to `#B08D57` with zero harsh glow rings.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 1px `#B08D57` outline with solid `#B08D57` check fills, keeping geometry neat and square (radii 2px for checkboxes).

### Tags & Badges
- Compact rectangular forms with `label-sm` font, uppercase lettering, and subtle backgrounds: `#E8E4DC` with `#1A1A1A` text for architectural specs, or `#3B4B3F` tint with soft green text for eco-luxury/Indochine distinctions.

### Material & Finish Swatches (Domain-Specific)
- Circular or square 48px material sample chips (representing Italian marble, Indochine woven rattan, smoked oak, brushed brass) enclosed in a 1px hairline border, expanding with an active champagne gold ring on selection.
